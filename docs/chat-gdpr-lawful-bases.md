# SanZa Chat — GDPR lawful-basis assessment (draft)

**Controller:** Abbas Akbari, sole trader trading as SanZa Food Hub. **Service:** SanZa Chat, 16+ moderated public chat. **Status:** documented assessment; not legal sign-off. **Review date:** 10 November 2026.

| Purpose | Personal data | Proposed Article 6 basis | Verification / action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provide nickname-based chat and show messages to other participants | Nickname, user/session ID, message, timestamp | Art 6(1)(b), performance of contract where necessary to deliver the requested service | Confirm necessity and transparent terms; distinguish optional features |
| Protect participants and stop spam/abuse | Technical identifiers, device/IP-derived security hashes, blocked messages, violation counts, ban records | Art 6(1)(f), legitimate interests in service safety | Document legitimate interests assessment (purpose, necessity, balancing, less intrusive alternatives), especially 16–17 year-olds |
| Review user reports, moderation and appeals | Reported content, reporter ID, moderation decisions, contact email when user writes in | Art 6(1)(f), legitimate interests in investigating harmful conduct | Document safeguards, human review and retention exceptions; assess legal obligations case by case |
| Respond to privacy-rights requests | Contact details, request, verification records | Art 6(1)(c), legal obligation, where GDPR requires a response | Minimise evidence; define retention for request records |
| Security logs and incident response | Event logs and technical identifiers | Art 6(1)(f) for security; Art 6(1)(c) only where a specific legal obligation applies | Identify logging provider, actual storage periods and incident procedure |
| Optional analytics, advertising or non-essential tracking (if enabled) | Cookies, device IDs, analytics events | Not assigned here | Audit scripts; assess Irish ePrivacy consent rules separately; do not activate non-essential tracking before compliance |

**Important:** Proposed bases require review against actual processing and contracts. No consent is presumed from merely joining chat. Do not process special-category information intentionally; users are instructed not to share it. Automated filters and bans must provide appeal/human-review paths. The 16+ gate is self-declared; safeguards for 16–17-year-olds must be reviewed. Document whether a DPIA is required. Record the legitimate-interests assessment before public launch.

**Data minimisation:** no DMs or user-uploaded images; no public email requirement for ordinary nickname chat. Reports should not send message text in email alerts. Verify implementation against this register.

**Retention:** proposed 30 days for ordinary messages and 90 days after resolution for reports, subject to verification of actual cleanup jobs, related records, legal holds, backups and service logs. These are not guarantees until confirmed.
