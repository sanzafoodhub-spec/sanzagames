What is SanZa Games?

SanZa Games is an entertainment project built around playful challenges, original characters and a distinctive SanZa identity. The collection brings together arcade-style action, a scooter adventure and light-hearted sound fun in one place.

This guide helps new players understand what each game is about, how to approach a challenge and what to look out for. Game features and controls can vary by device and game version, so always check the instructions shown when a game opens.

Running challenge

1. SanZa Run

SanZa Run is a fast-paced running game inspired by a journey through Dublin. Players must react to obstacles, choose a safe path and collect in-game items while trying to keep their run going.

Beginner tips

  • Look ahead rather than focusing only on your character.
  • Pay attention to obstacles and decide early when to change lanes.
  • Learn when jumping or sliding is useful by following the game's prompts.
  • Collectible items can add an extra goal, but staying safe is more important than chasing every item.
  • If you lose a life, notice which obstacle caused it and try a different approach next time.

Challenge idea: Play a few rounds and see whether you can improve your distance while making fewer mistakes.

Scooter adventure

2. Yupbro Scooty

Yupbro Scooty takes the SanZa spirit onto a scooter, with an urban route and collectible brownies. The challenge is to keep moving, react to the route and avoid obstacles as you travel.

Beginner tips

  • Give yourself time to react when the route becomes busy.
  • Look for a clear path before moving toward a collectible.
  • Follow the on-screen control instructions for your device.
  • Use each attempt to learn where you tend to make mistakes.
  • Enjoy the journey as well as the score; improving control is part of the fun.

Challenge idea: Try to collect more brownies on your next run without taking unnecessary risks.

Arcade action

3. SanZa Smash

SanZa Smash brings arcade-style smashing action into the collection. Players aim for targets, time their moves and work through the challenge while watching what happens on screen.

Beginner tips

  • Read the instructions shown in the game before starting.
  • Wait until a target is in a suitable position before making a move.
  • Pay attention to the target counter and any changes in the challenge.
  • Watch for changes in the action as you progress.
  • If a move misses, slow down and focus on timing rather than repeating it quickly.

Challenge idea: Aim for accurate hits and learn the timing of the targets as you play.

Funny sound fun

4. Funny Keys

Funny Keys adds a lighter experience to the SanZa collection. It is designed around playful sounds and silly moments rather than a traditional running or action challenge.

Ways to enjoy it

  • Explore the available buttons and discover the sounds they trigger.
  • Try different combinations to create your own funny sound sequence.
  • Keep the volume at a comfortable level, especially when using headphones.
  • Share the fun with friends or family when appropriate.
Explore Funny Keys

Mobile gaming tips

  • Check the controls: Different games may use different touch controls.
  • Keep your screen clear: Close overlays or menus that block important parts of the game.
  • Check your connection: A stable internet connection can help browser games load properly.
  • Take breaks: Pause regularly, especially during longer sessions.
  • Play fairly: Focus on improving your own skills rather than trying to exploit bugs.
