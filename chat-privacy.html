SanZa Chat — Privacy Notice (Draft for Review)

This is a private-beta draft, not a declaration of GDPR compliance. Public launch is on hold pending verification of retention periods, legal bases, suppliers, security, and children's safeguards.

Who to contact

SanZa Chat / SanZa Games: Sanzafoodhub@gmail.com. The operator's full legal identity and postal contact must be confirmed before launch.

What information the chat uses

Chosen nickname, chat messages, user/session identifiers, moderation reports, enforcement records and technical/security information such as IP-derived identifiers may be processed. Other participants can see posted messages and nicknames. Do not share real names, addresses, schools, phone numbers, passwords or other identifying details.

Why

To operate the public chat, protect members, detect abuse, investigate reports and enforce safety rules. The specific GDPR lawful basis for each activity, including children's data, is under assessment and must be documented before launch.

Who handles data

The chat uses infrastructure including Supabase and Vercel. Provider locations, international transfers and contractual protections require verification before launch.

Retention and deletion — proposed preview policy

For the private beta, the operator has approved a proposed 30-day retention period for ordinary chat messages and 90 days after resolution for resolved moderation reports. Messages linked to pending reports are retained while they are under review. Evidence needed for an ongoing investigation, dispute or legal obligation may be retained longer where necessary and lawful.

Important: These are proposed periods, not yet active automatic deletion guarantees. Scheduled cleanup has not been enabled or verified. The handling of associated messages, resolved reports, account identifiers, bans, device-security records and infrastructure logs requires further review and implementation before public launch. Deleting a browser session does not erase server-stored records. Users may request erasure using the contact details above, subject to applicable law.

Your rights

Contact the email above to request access, correction, erasure or to raise a privacy concern, subject to applicable law. You may also complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission: dataprotection.ie.

Children

Under 13s cannot join. Ages 13–15 are currently blocked until appropriate safeguards and parental permission arrangements are implemented. The 16+ age selector is self-declared, not proof of age. Do not treat this preview as an age-verified service.
