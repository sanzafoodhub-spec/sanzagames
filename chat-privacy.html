SanZa Chat — Privacy Notice (Draft for Review)

This is a private-beta draft, not a declaration of GDPR compliance. Public launch is on hold pending verification of retention periods, legal bases, suppliers, security, and children's safeguards.

Who to contact

SanZa Chat and SanZa Games are operated by an individual sole trader, trading under the registered business name SanZa Food Hub. Business correspondence address: The Dance Factory, Century Business Park, Finglas, Dublin, D11 VP46, Ireland. The sole trader’s full legal name and any additional required business disclosures must be confirmed before public launch. This address was supplied by the operator for public business correspondence; no private residential address is published. Contact: Sanzafoodhub@gmail.com. The operator is not represented here as a limited company.

What information the chat uses

Chosen nickname, chat messages, user/session identifiers, moderation reports, enforcement records and technical/security information such as IP-derived identifiers may be processed. Other participants can see posted messages and nicknames. Do not share real names, addresses, schools, phone numbers, passwords or other identifying details.

Why

To operate the public chat, protect members, detect abuse, investigate reports and enforce safety rules. The GDPR lawful basis must be assessed and recorded for each purpose before launch. Possible bases include providing the requested chat service and legitimate interests in preventing abuse, but no basis has yet been finally approved; these are not claims of compliance.

Who handles data

The chat uses infrastructure including Supabase and Vercel. Provider locations, international transfers and contractual protections require verification before launch.

Retention and deletion — proposed preview policy

For the private beta, the operator has approved a proposed 30-day retention period for ordinary chat messages and 90 days after resolution for resolved moderation reports. Messages linked to pending reports are retained while they are under review. Evidence needed for an ongoing investigation, dispute or legal obligation may be retained longer where necessary and lawful.

Important: These are proposed periods, not yet active automatic deletion guarantees. Scheduled database cleanup jobs have been configured for ordinary messages and resolved reports, but successful execution and coverage of all related records have not yet been independently verified. The handling of associated messages, resolved reports, account identifiers, bans, device-security records and infrastructure logs requires further review and implementation before public launch. Deleting a browser session does not erase server-stored records. Users may request erasure using the contact details above, subject to applicable law.

Your privacy choices and rights

You can request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to certain processing. Other rights may apply depending on the processing and lawful basis. Email sanzafoodhub@gmail.com with the subject SanZa Chat Privacy Request. Tell us your chat nickname and what you want us to review. Do not email passwords, authentication codes, or identity documents unless we specifically explain why further verification is necessary.

We may need to verify that you control the account before releasing or deleting records. We aim to respond without undue delay and normally within one month, subject to lawful extensions. Deletion requests are reviewed individually; content subject to active investigations or legal obligations may need to be retained. Closing the browser does not delete server records.

You may complain to Ireland’s Data Protection Commission: dataprotection.ie.

Cookies, device identifiers and security

SanZa Chat uses session and technical information to maintain access, operate abuse-prevention controls and support moderation. Cloudflare Turnstile is used to help distinguish automated traffic; Supabase and Vercel provide authentication, storage and hosting services. The exact cookies, local-storage items, IP-derived or device-linked identifiers, their retention periods, and any optional tracking must be checked against the deployed implementation before public launch. Do not use this notice as a claim that no cookies or tracking technologies are used.

Underage access and safety

Anyone who declares that they are under 16 is refused access to the chat. A false age declaration breaches the chat rules. If we reasonably suspect a participant is under 16, we may restrict access, investigate the report and remove information where appropriate, subject to legal retention obligations. Age selection is self-declared and does not independently verify age.

Children

Only people aged 16 or older may join this private preview. Under-16 users who disclose their age are blocked by the age selection, but self-declaration is not proof of age. Users aged 16–17 are still children under Irish data protection law and need age-appropriate safeguards. Do not share identifying information. Do not treat this preview as an age-verified service.
