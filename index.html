YOUR GUIDE TO THE ARCADE

How to Play SanZa Games 🎮

New to SanZa Games? Start by choosing a game from the collection above. Each adventure has its own objectives, characters and challenges. Follow the instructions shown when the game loads, as controls can vary between games.

🏃 Master SanZa Run

Watch for obstacles, plan your next move early and collect items when it is safe.

🛴 Take on Yupbro Scooty

Keep an eye on the path ahead, avoid obstacles and collect brownies without losing control.

💥 Try SanZa Smash

Read the on-screen instructions, pay attention to targets and timing, and watch for changes as you progress.

📱 Tips for playing on a phone

Use a stable internet connection and follow each game's control prompts. Allow a game to finish loading before trying again.
