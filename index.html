MEET THE SANZA UNIVERSE

More than just a game 🎮

SanZa Games is a family entertainment project inspired by the SanZa community in Dublin. Explore browser-based games, meet memorable characters and discover different challenges across the SanZa universe. SanZa Run is about timing and collecting; Yupbro Scooty brings a scooter adventure; SanZa Smash adds cartoon-style smashing and cookie-themed chaos; Cat in the Hat brings a dodge-and-attack challenge; and Funny Keys is all about playful sounds.

To get started, choose a game above and follow the instructions shown inside it. Controls and objectives differ between games, and some games open on a separate website. On a phone, keep your screen clear, use a stable internet connection and allow the game to finish loading before playing.

🏃 SanZa Run tips

Watch the path ahead, plan your moves early and collect items when it is safe. Use each attempt to improve your timing.

🛴 Yupbro Scooty tips

Stay alert, avoid obstacles and look for brownies. Learn the route and focus on keeping your run going.

💥 SanZa Smash tips

Read the on-screen instructions, pay attention to targets and counters, and focus on accurate moves rather than rushing.

🎹 Funny Keys tips

Try the available keys, discover different sounds and experiment with your own silly combinations.
