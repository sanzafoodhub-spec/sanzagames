SanZa Chat — Rules & Terms (Private Beta Draft)

These draft rules apply to invited preview testing. Public terms, operator details and applicable procedures must be legally reviewed before launch.

Operator and contact

SanZa Chat is operated by Abbas Akbari, sole trader trading as SanZa Food Hub, The Dance Factory, Century Business Park, Finglas, Dublin, D11 VP46, Ireland. Contact: sanzafoodhub@gmail.com.

Age and access

SanZa Chat is strictly for people aged 16 or older. Anyone under 16 must not join. Age is self-declared and cannot be independently verified by the age selector. Participants aged 16–17 must follow the same safety rules and should avoid sharing identifying details.

Be safe and respectful

No bullying, hate speech, sexual content, grooming, threats, encouragement of self-harm, scams, spam, impersonation or attempts to evade bans. No links, contact information, personal details, private messages, photos or files. Do not ask anyone for their age, school, location, passwords or off-platform contact.

Public messages

Messages and nicknames can be seen by other chat participants. Do not share anything private. Automated filters can miss harmful content or block harmless messages.

Moderation and bans

SanZa may reject messages, review reports and suspend accounts, including 48-hour bans for repeated rule violations. Report abuse using the chat's report control or email Sanzafoodhub@gmail.com. To appeal a moderation action, email the same address with the nickname and approximate time, without sending sensitive personal details.

Emergencies

This is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Safety reports must have a staffed response procedure before public launch.
