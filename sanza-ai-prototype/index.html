Privacy, age & safety information

SanZa is an AI, not a person. This preview is not approved for use by children. It uses third-party AI services (Groq and Google Gemini). Your question and recent conversation may be sent to the provider that answers. Providers may process or retain data under their own terms. SanZa does not intentionally store chat history on its server; this page keeps recent conversation in memory until refresh or New Chat. Do not enter names, contact details, addresses, passwords, school information, health details or other sensitive data.

Microphone questions use your browser speech recognition service, which may send audio to its provider. SanZa does not intentionally save audio. The microphone must be activated by you and can be stopped by tapping it again. You can always type instead.

AI responses can be inaccurate or unsuitable despite safeguards. If you see harmful content, stop using the preview and report it to the SanZa operator. For emergencies contact local emergency services or a trusted adult. No private messaging with SanZa staff is offered through this preview.
