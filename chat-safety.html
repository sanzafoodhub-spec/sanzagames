SanZa Chat — Safety & Reporting

Private beta: safeguards are being tested. Never rely solely on automatic filtering. This page does not mean the chat is ready for children.

How to report

Use REPORT MESSAGE in chat to flag a specific message. For privacy, abuse, safeguarding or moderation appeals email Sanzafoodhub@gmail.com. Include the approximate time, nickname and what happened, without sending personal information about children.

If you feel unsafe

Stop responding, use BLOCK NICKNAME, leave the chat and tell a trusted adult. Never move conversations to other apps or share contact details.

Serious harm

Threats, grooming, sexual exploitation, or risk of immediate harm need prompt human assessment. The chat is not monitored continuously by a person. For immediate danger contact emergency services; in Ireland call 112 or 999.

Parents and guardians

Under 13s cannot join. Ages 13–15 are not admitted to this beta. Please supervise children's internet use and encourage them to tell you about concerning interactions.
